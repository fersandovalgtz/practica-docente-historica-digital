# Code of conduct

PDHD is a scholarly and technical collaboration space. Participation should protect both people and the integrity of the research record.

## Expected conduct

Contributors should communicate respectfully, distinguish evidence from interpretation, document uncertainty, credit prior work, respect source and data rights, and critique methods or claims without personal attacks.

Scientific disagreement is welcome when it is evidence-based and reviewable. Deliberate fabrication, provenance erasure, plagiarism, harassment, discriminatory conduct, credential exposure, or attempts to bypass human-validation safeguards are incompatible with participation.

## Research-specific expectations

Do not pressure collaborators to force sample targets, suppress unresolved conflicts, relabel model output as human work, or redistribute restricted source material. When evidence is insufficient, the correct state is unresolved, blocked or unknown.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, reject, close or remove contributions and participation that violate these expectations. Serious security or safety concerns should be reported privately according to `SECURITY.md`.
