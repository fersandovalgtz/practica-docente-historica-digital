# Rights and reuse policy

PDHD is a research infrastructure, not a republication mirror. Discovery of a digital source does not imply a right to redistribute it.

## Rights classes

Every source and document must receive one of these operational classes before content processing is published:

- `metadata_only`: public repository may retain identifiers, bibliographic metadata, URLs, locators, rights notes and original non-infringing PDHD annotations; source images and full OCR are excluded.
- `local_processing_only`: restricted material may be processed in a non-versioned local environment for legitimate research purposes, subject to source terms; outputs are published only when legally permissible.
- `redistributable_with_attribution`: source terms permit redistribution under stated attribution or license conditions.
- `public_domain_verified`: public-domain status has been affirmatively checked and documented; jurisdiction and basis must be recorded.
- `permission_granted`: written permission exists for the specific use; permission evidence must be preserved outside public data if it contains personal or administrative information.
- `review_required`: rights status is unresolved; no source content is committed.

## HNDM default

The Hemeroteca Nacional Digital de México is assigned `metadata_only` by default. Its published conditions state that digital contents are protected, use is for personal or academic purposes, attribution is required, and digital images may not be included in systems, applications or other environments without prior written authorization.

Institutional reference: <https://hndm.iib.unam.mx/index.php/es/tramites-y-servicios?start=1>

Therefore, unless a more permissive status is documented for a specific object, PDHD does not commit HNDM facsimiles, page images or full OCR to the public repository.

## No rights laundering

A transformation such as OCR, format conversion, compression, transcription or embedding does not by itself create a new right to redistribute the underlying protected content.

## Attribution

When a source requires attribution, PDHD records the exact preferred credit line when available and preserves the original source URL or identifier.
