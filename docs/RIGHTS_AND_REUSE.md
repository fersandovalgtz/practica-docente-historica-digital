# Rights and reuse operational guide

Before committing any page image, facsimile, OCR text or transcription derived from a third-party source, answer these questions:

1. Who supplies the digital object?
2. What rights or use statement applies to the specific object or collection?
3. Does public access also permit redistribution?
4. Does the permission cover images, text extraction, OCR, derivatives and machine-readable publication, or only consultation?
5. Is attribution required?
6. Is written permission needed?
7. Can PDHD publish only metadata and analytical annotations instead?

When any answer is unclear, use `review_required` or `metadata_only` rather than assuming permission.

## HNDM

Default: `metadata_only`. Publicly versioned data may include bibliographic metadata, stable locators, rights notes, project-created annotations and code. Do not add HNDM page images or full OCR unless specific written authorization or another documented legal basis covers that use.

## Aggregators

Repository-level openness does not imply that every aggregated object has the same license. For the Repositorio Institucional de la UNAM, check the contributing repository and object-level rights information when content redistribution is contemplated.
