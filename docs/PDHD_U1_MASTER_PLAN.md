# PDHD-U1 master plan 0.1

**Title:** Genealogía documental de la práctica docente mexicana

## Purpose

Build the first controlled research universe for PDHD and test the full chain from source discovery to historically defensible analysis.

## Target size

First stable cohort: **50–100 documents**. Scale is deliberately limited until identity, rights, annotation and validation rules have been tested.

## Periodization frame

- 1870–1940: pedagogical press, normalism, inspection, methods, discipline and professionalization.
- 1921–1970: post-revolutionary teacher, rural schooling, campaigns and state pedagogical policy.
- 1970–2000: planning, technification, evaluation and professional development.
- 2000–2026: inclusion, competencies, technologies, institutional evaluation and Nueva Escuela Mexicana.

The periods overlap intentionally at turning points. They are analytical frames, not assumptions of abrupt historical rupture.

## Phase 1 — Documentary census

Create a candidate register from HNDM, UNAM and SEP. Record title, date, institutional locator, documentary type, period and initial rights status.

**Exit criterion:** at least 50 candidates with usable identity and no undocumented source substitution.

## Phase 2 — Rights triage

Resolve every candidate into an operational rights class. No source facsimile or full OCR enters Git history while status is `review_required`.

**Exit criterion:** 100% of selected documents have an explicit rights status.

## Phase 3 — Fragment pilot

Select a stratified subset and annotate pedagogical fragments using the 0.1 dimension and act taxonomies.

**Exit criterion:** enough fragments exist to test ambiguity, inter-annotator rules and the distinction between prescription and practice.

## Phase 4 — Human validation

Review candidate annotations and document disagreement. Revise taxonomy only through versioned changes.

**Exit criterion:** a validated benchmark subset exists and its construction is documented.

## Phase 5 — First analyses

Prioritize longitudinal questions that the corpus can actually support: shifts in pedagogical acts, authority, assessment language, discipline, teacher identity and relations between regulation and practice discourse.

## Release criterion for v0.1.0

- stable source and document schema;
- rights registry complete for the released cohort;
- frozen taxonomies;
- documented annotation protocol;
- validated pilot subset;
- reproducible integrity checks;
- release notes that distinguish technical coverage from semantic validation.
