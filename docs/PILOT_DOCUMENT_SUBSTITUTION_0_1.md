# PDHD pilot document substitution 0.1

Decision date: **2026-09-07**

Pilot selection position 13 changes from `PDHD-D000053` (*El Maestro*, tomo II, núm. 3, December 1921) to `PDHD-D000056` (*El Maestro*, tomo II, núm. 1, October 1921).

The outgoing issue was directly inspected. Its candidate *El Cardo* and the remaining OCR-assisted lexical survivors failed the deterministic slot-A requirement because they did not contain a defensible explicit pedagogical action or instructional prescription. Freezing such a passage would have weakened the protocol.

The replacement was selected only after direct primary inspection. BookReader `n4` identifies tomo II, núm. I, October 1921; `n5` independently identifies the Secretaría de Educación Pública / Talleres Gráficos imprint and México, October 1921; printed p. 57 / `n60` supplies a source-critical passage in Gabriela Mistral's letter to José Vasconcelos; and printed p. 83 / `n86` in *La enseñanza del dibujo* contains an explicit sequence of teacher and pupil actions.

The substitution has no effect on pilot stratification: era remains E3, place remains México, documentary type remains `issue`, and *El Maestro* remains four of the 24 selected documents. Fragment IDs `PDHD-F000049`–`PDHD-F000052` remain stable because they are deterministically tied to selection position 13.

The outgoing December discoveries remain in provenance-oriented records without active pilot-fragment promotion links. The machine-readable decision is stored in `data/samples/pilot_document_substitutions_0_1.csv`.
