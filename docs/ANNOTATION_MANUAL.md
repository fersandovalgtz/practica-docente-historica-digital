# Annotation manual 0.1

## Unit of annotation

Annotate the smallest fragment that retains enough context to identify a pedagogically meaningful act, norm, representation or institutional relation. Do not reduce the fragment so far that the historical meaning depends on omitted clauses.

## Dimension

Assign one primary `dimension_code` from `data/taxonomy/pedagogical_dimensions.csv`. Secondary dimensions may be introduced later; version 0.1 prioritizes consistent primary coding.

## Pedagogical act

Assign a `pedagogical_act` only when an action is explicitly present or strongly recoverable from the wording. Do not infer an act merely because a document belongs to a pedagogical genre.

## Normativity

Use:

- `prescriptive` for duties, instructions, recommendations or norms.
- `descriptive` for descriptions not presented as direct reports of practice.
- `reported_practice` when an actor or document reports that a practice occurred.
- `observed_practice` only when the evidentiary setting supports contemporaneous observation.
- `analytical` for historiographical or conceptual discussion about practice.
- `unclear` when the evidence does not permit a defensible choice.

## Validation

Automated or provisional coding is `unvalidated`. A human reviewer may mark it `human_validated`, `rejected` or `needs_review`. Human validation refers to the annotation, not to the historical truth of every statement made by the source.
