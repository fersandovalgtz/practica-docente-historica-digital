# PDHD-U1 calibration protocol 0.1

Status: **prepared, not started**  
Preparation date: **2026-09-07**

## Purpose

This protocol operationalizes the transition from the completed 96/96 frozen fragment package to the first human-validation stage. Calibration is a training and codebook-refinement stage. It is not part of the formal independent reliability estimate.

The governing distinction remains:

`fragment_freezing != annotation != human_validation`

## Calibration population

The calibration set contains **12 fragments drawn from the 96 frozen pilot fragments**. These 12 fragments are excluded from the later independent reliability calculation. Once calibration is completed and the pilot codebook is frozen, the remaining **84 fragments** form the first blind fixed-span reliability batch unless a later versioned methodological decision changes that design.

The calibration selection is generated by `scripts/build_calibration_package.py` and stored in `data/samples/calibration_manifest_0_1.csv`.

## Selection rule

Selection uses structural metadata only. It does **not** inspect model predictions, proposed human labels, preparation notes or historical source text.

The fixed seed is:

`PDHD-CAL-0.1-20260907`

The deterministic constraints are:

- 12 distinct documents;
- era quotas E1=5, E3=6 and E4=1;
- exactly three fragments from each deterministic slot A, B, C and D;
- at least two documents from regional origins outside the Mexico-City place labels used in the pilot table;
- at least five documentary types;
- no publication contributes more than two calibration items;
- the E3 subset must include an institutional monograph, an official report and at least one teacher-guidance or policy-proposal object.

Among combinations satisfying those constraints, the script selects the minimum SHA-256 structural score. Slot assignment and presentation order are also deterministic SHA-256 operations under the same seed.

The resulting manifest is therefore reproducible but not semantically cherry-picked.

## Blinding

Researchers may inspect `calibration_manifest_0_1.csv`; coders should not use it during the coding session because it contains era and A-D slot metadata.

Coders receive only `data/samples/calibration_coder_sheet_0_1.csv` plus access to the cited primary evidence. The coder sheet deliberately omits:

- era;
- A-D slot;
- selection role;
- semantic locator slug;
- preparation note;
- freeze status;
- researcher/model labels.

The sheet uses randomized `CAL001`-`CAL012` item IDs and neutral structural boundary instructions. Source wording needed to identify the fixed span may be viewed in the primary object, but no proposed coding decision is supplied.

## Codebook state

Calibration uses baseline version:

`PDHD-CB-0.2-calibration`

This is an exact snapshot of the current annotation manual and controlled taxonomies, pinned by Git blob SHA in `data/samples/codebook_registry.csv`.

The baseline is **not** the independent-round frozen codebook. After the two coders complete an initial independent pass on the 12 calibration items, they discuss disagreements. Definitions and examples may then be revised through a versioned change.

The codebook used for formal reliability must be registered separately as a frozen pilot version before any of the 84 reliability fragments are independently coded. A definition change during the independent round invalidates the affected reliability snapshot.

## Human procedure

At least two human coders are required. At least one should ideally not have participated in construction of the taxonomy.

For calibration:

1. Assign each coder a stable pseudonymous `coder_id`.
2. Give each coder an independent local copy of the blind coder sheet.
3. Each coder completes all 12 items without discussing individual answers during the first pass.
4. Freeze those first-pass calibration responses as provenance before discussion.
5. Compare disagreements field by field. Calibration disagreement is diagnostic; it is not the formal reliability result.
6. Revise definitions, boundary rules or examples only where disagreement reveals a genuine codebook problem.
7. Record every change in version control.
8. Register and freeze the independent-round codebook.
9. Generate the 84-item blind reliability sheets, explicitly excluding the 12 calibration fragment IDs.

## Rights and source handling

This repository stores identifiers, URLs and neutral boundary instructions. It does not republish third-party facsimiles or full historical transcriptions when the rights basis is metadata-only or otherwise restricted.

Coder-local source text or page images required for practical coding remain outside public Git history unless a stronger reuse basis is documented.

## Gate to independent reliability

The project may move from calibration to formal reliability only when all of the following are true:

- two human first-pass calibration sheets are complete;
- disagreements have been reviewed;
- any codebook revisions are versioned;
- a new independent-round codebook version is registered and frozen;
- the 12 calibration IDs are excluded from the reliability manifest;
- coder-facing reliability sheets contain no researcher/model labels or selection-role metadata;
- validation scripts pass.

Until then, the correct public status is **calibration prepared, human coding not started**.
