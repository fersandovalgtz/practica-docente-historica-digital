# Source policy

## Inclusion criteria

A document is eligible for PDHD when it contributes evidence about teaching practice, teacher formation, pedagogical regulation, classroom organization, assessment, discipline, materials, professional identity or adjacent processes directly shaping the act of teaching in Mexico.

Priority is given to documents with a stable institutional locator, interpretable date, sufficient bibliographic identity and a rights status that can be documented.

## Exclusion or retention without processing

A discovered item may be catalogued but withheld from content processing when its identity is ambiguous, the source link is unstable, the rights status is unresolved, the scan is unusable or the item is merely duplicative of another better-defined object.

## Source families

Initial families are HNDM, the Repositorio Institucional de la UNAM and historical/official SEP collections. Additional archives may be incorporated only after their access conditions and documentary role are documented.

## No silent substitution

If a preferred institutional source becomes unavailable, a mirror or secondary copy must not silently replace it. The new provenance relationship must be recorded.
